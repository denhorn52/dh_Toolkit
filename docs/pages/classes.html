6.00 Classes:

dh_Toolkit defines a set of modified Lokasenna classes. The dh_Toolkit classes are prefixed with "dh_". These pages will outline the differences between them. Most of the modifications involve renaming of some properties, the changing of default colors, and in some cases some added functionality. For more complete documentation on the usage of the Lokasenna GUI visit its project wiki.

Note: Most of the dh_Toolkit classes expose all of their color properties so that the developer can tweak them to suit his or her needs. However, that is not always advised. dh_Toolkit was designed to use themes. Caution must be exercised when varying from the defaults. What may look good with one theme may not look good with another.
